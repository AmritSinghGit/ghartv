GHARTV · JIO LIVE — DAILY USE

1. Open Apps > GharTV · Jio Live.
2. Use arrows to highlight a live channel.
3. Press OK to watch.
4. Type a channel number to tune directly.
5. Press Channel + or Channel - to switch.
6. Press GUIDE (or TV) to return to the live guide.
7. Press INFO while watching for Now and Next.
8. Hold OK on a channel card to add or remove a Favourite.

ACCOUNT / UPDATE
Open Jio account only to update the guide, check for a GharTV update, or reconnect Jio.
