GHARTV · JIO LIVE — DAILY USE

1. Open Apps > GharTV · Jio Live.
2. Start from Home, Recent, Favourites, All channels, Punjabi, Hindi, English or another language/genre.
3. Press OK on a channel to watch.
4. The Now/Next panel disappears automatically.
5. Press INFO or OK while watching to show programme progress, Previous, Guide and Next.
6. Channel + / Channel - stays inside the view you chose.
7. Type a channel number to tune directly from the complete Jio guide.
8. Press GUIDE (or TV) to return home.

SUBSCRIPTION / NEEDS ATTENTION
- Choose Next working channel to continue immediately.
- Subscription means Jio explicitly marked the account/channel as requiring a plan.
- Needs attention means Jio continued to deny or could not play that channel; other channels may still work.

UPDATE
Open Account > Check for GharTV update. Android updates the same app and keeps the saved login, favourites and local suggestions.

RECALL
------
After changing channels, choose Recall to return to the previous channel. Choose it again to toggle back.
