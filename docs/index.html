A few things worth knowing

Before you press play.

Already have GharTV? How do I update?

Open GharTV, go to Jio account, and choose Check for GharTV update. Download and confirm installation when Android asks. Keep the existing app installed; there is no need to uninstall it or clear its data.

Will it work on my smart TV?

The app is for Android TV and Google TV. TVs with a different operating system cannot install this app directly. You can use a compatible external Android TV or Google TV streaming device instead.

Does GharTV include free access to every channel?

No. You need your own eligible provider account. The provider decides which channels are available to you. GharTV does not unlock subscriptions or bypass content protection.

Why does an older programme have black bars?

Some programmes have a different shape from your screen. Fit keeps the original proportions; Crop fills the screen by cutting off some of the image; Stretch fills it by changing the proportions. Resizing is not the same as adding genuine picture detail.

What information does the app send?

Technical diagnostics are optional. Use Diagnostics & privacy inside the app to review your settings. Never share your OTP or account credentials. Read the privacy information for details.

Can I watch directly on this website?

This website is for learning about GharTV and downloading the TV app. It does not stream channels. Open GharTV on your compatible television to watch.
