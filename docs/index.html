02 / GET STARTED

From this page to your TV.

An Android TV / Google TV device, internet access and your own eligible Jio account. A smart TV running another operating system cannot install this Android APK.

  1. Get the APK onto your TV.

    Open this page in a TV-compatible browser/download tool, or transfer the APK from your phone or computer with a file-transfer tool you trust.

  2. Confirm the Android installation.

    If prompted, allow that specific download/file tool to install unknown apps. Choose Install. You can turn off that temporary permission afterwards.

  3. Connect inside GharTV.

    Open the app and use your Jio mobile number and OTP. Do not send your OTP to anyone. Browse the channels available to your account.

  1. Check inside the existing app.

    GharTV → Jio account → Check for GharTV update → Download update.

  2. Allow and install the update.

    If Android asks, allow GharTV to install updates, return and check again. Confirm Install, then Open.

  3. Keep your existing data.

    Do not uninstall or clear app storage. The current release checks automatically, but does not force or silently install an update.

05 / THE USEFUL DETAILS

Before you reach for the remote.

Does GharTV make every channel free?

No. Use your own eligible Jio account. Channels, subscriptions, device support and playback are controlled by the provider. GharTV does not unlock subscriptions or bypass content protection.

Why are some playback buttons missing?

Pause, seek, Live and catch-up depend on what the channel exposes. A live feed without a time-shift window cannot be treated like a recorded programme.

Why did the app ask about diagnostics?

Technical reporting is optional. Jio account → Diagnostics & privacy lets you review and manage it. The current policy excludes OTPs, credentials, stream URLs, precise location and successful programme-viewing history. Read the privacy details.

Is this a general public build?

This is an unofficial household release. Its existing APK includes household birthday personalisation and a family photograph. Those packaged assets are not made private by turning off diagnostics. Household assets need separation before a broader public release.

Can a low-resolution channel become Full HD or 4K?

A larger output image is not the same as a better source. First prefer an authorised HD rendition when the channel provides one. Resizing alone does not recover detail that the source never contained.

The review candidate reports source pixels, display mode and bitrate/bandwidth separately. It does not implement AI super-resolution or promise native 4K from an SD feed.

Which shape should I choose for a 4:3 programme?

Fit preserves everything. Zoom fills a 16:9 screen by cropping the top and bottom. Stretch fills it without discarding the frame, but makes objects wider. For logos, captions and faces, the undistorted fit view is the safer default.

If black bars are encoded inside the source video itself, the player needs a separate crop treatment; this screenshot alone cannot establish that.

Will the app update automatically?

The current updater checks automatically with a 12-hour interval limit. A manual check is immediate. Installation still requires Android confirmation; compulsory and silent updates are not implemented.

What if playback or installation fails?

Confirm internet access and the account’s channel availability. Use the app’s retry/reconnect or Guide controls. For installation, check the permission for the specific installer. Never post your OTP, account credentials or private dashboard token in a public issue.
