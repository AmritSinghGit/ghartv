# Security and limitations

- The OTP is never persisted.
- Jio session material is encrypted through Android Keystore.
- Cleartext network traffic and Android application backup are disabled.
- The application does not bundle a third-party or repackaged JioTV APK.
- It does not provide DRM keys, unlock subscriptions or bypass geographic/device policy.
- Jio may change private interfaces, token rules, channel availability or playback requirements without notice.
- Some channels require a valid JioTV subscription attached to the connected account.
- A virtual Google TV device may not reproduce a retail television's Widevine security level or HDCP path.
- The physical Hisense E6N test is therefore the acceptance gate for protected playback.
- GharTV is unofficial and is not supported or endorsed by Jio.
