# GharTV v0.5.4 RC5 owner review

RC5 is the compile-corrected continuation of RC4. It keeps the long living-room tile, adds automatic bounded preview, and introduces a real birthday launch splash. The owner-supplied family photo is used only for Dad and Simrat, the two people represented in the photo.

## Review gates

1. Dad birthday launch shows the family photo and advances automatically or on OK.
2. Simrat is spelled correctly everywhere and 4 October selects Simrat.
3. Standard mode opens without the birthday photo.
4. Highlighting a normal channel starts muted preview after a short dwell; fast navigation does not stream every card.
5. Preview stops within 15 seconds and moving focus releases it.
6. OK on the preview, the card, or Watch Live opens continuous playback.
7. Working, Subscription, and Needs attention use distinct colours and copy.
8. Voice/programme search, Recall, scoped channel surfing, catch-up and transport controls remain functional.
9. Stable update remains v0.5.3 until owner approval.
